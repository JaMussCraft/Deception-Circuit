# Independent evidence critique — before cue outcomes

2026-10-21. Evidence-only synthesis of the five SDR reports, semantic/error audits and frozen cue spec; no model execution, intervention proposal, source change or release. Lead compactions **5**; worker compactions excluded. Claims below concern reported evidence, not an independent raw-artifact reanalysis.

## Judgment

**The methods suffice for the present scientific question; the behavioral variable is not yet identified well enough for the intended deception mechanism story.** Existing interventions localize conditional answer-processing effects. Better instrumentation cannot turn an ambiguous selection endpoint into a pressure-specific causal variable. A narrower reference-selection mechanism could become worthwhile, but requires its own predictive evidence and an explicit change of claim.

## What survives the original role/overlap hypotheses

**Quantitative evidence:** D/H/F contain 69/56/68 heads; triple32 knockout leaves native deceptive-choice accuracy .002. D−F33 leaves .119 versus matched-null p5 .517; D−H−F22 leaves .782 versus p5 .733. Shared43 sufficiency is .494; adding D-exclusive heads restores .967, retaining every MLP and the specified replacement background. Triple32 contains all six leading candidate senders and both nominated downstream heads. External D−F deception is .650 versus its random mean .618; reviewed triple32 external evidence is absent.

**Rejected readings:** “All differences are weak” is contradicted by native D−F. “Exclusive heads are irrelevant” is contradicted by their conditional sufficiency contribution. Subtraction does not isolate a demonstrated deception module: it removes the leading candidates. Near-zero knockout endpoints cannot rank specificity or establish redundancy.

**Unsupported, still plausible hypotheses:** shared computation contributes to both truthful and deceptive selection; exclusive heads provide conditional context or execution support; early candidates alter downstream selection; late heads transport or select answer information. Membership and D/H knockout asymmetries do not identify these functions. Autonomous exclusive-module accounts remain unsupported, not falsified; weak knockout effects can coexist with redundancy or compensation. D-specific processing remains possible within a shared head or subspace, but no current result establishes it. F's larger deception reduction (.777 versus D .723), alongside its narrowly greater collateral NLL rise (.668 versus .611), supports a utility tradeoff rather than demonstrated D specificity.

## Assay alternatives: serious, incomplete explanations

**Quantitative evidence:** native controls yielded neutral reporting 337/512 and retrieval 1392/1536, failing the frozen thresholds. Reporting follows the jointly first record/question/example candidate in 1305/1536 cases; 563/589 wrong reports occur first-harmless. There are 494 wrong-report/correct-retrieval pairs. Conflicting C−A is +24/256, exactly offset by −24/256 in aligned cases; wording-specific conflicting differences are +28/128 and −4/128. The separate completion diagnostic recovered 512/512 factual retrieval answers after an earlier first-token failure.

**Inference:** reporting is strongly sensitive to task/scaffold cues, while capability failure is not a single global inability. The record audit found no gold or renderer inversion. Choosing a harmless item to show violates the reporting norm but need not assert a false fact. Correct retrieval in a different task does not establish the internal state during reporting.

**Hypothesis:** shared bottlenecks for reference binding, answer formatting or selection could explain powerful overlapping-mask knockouts, positional replacement effects, and weaker external transfer when scaffolds/donors change. This is a coherent alternative, **not an established explanation** of the original masks. No matched mediation evidence links the later behavioral errors to those original knockout effects. Native/external differences also mix tasks, donors, selection histories and endpoints. Neither “artifact explains everything” nor “external transfer validates a deception mechanism” follows.

## What the paths establish

**Quantitative evidence:** same-false-donor candidate6 replacement raises truth margin +1.71. Confirmation effects include L13H20→L17Kgroup6 +.201, L13H21→Kgroup6 +.181, L12H6→Kgroup6 +.147, and L13H5→L17H24Q +.096. Discovery signs agree. Shared32 correction of 34/48 pressured answers accompanies loss of 36/37 accessible honest answers. Candidate/selector joint replacement has interaction −.740.

**Supported inference:** these interfaces transmit the specified perturbations; emitted donor-item copying is insufficient as a universal explanation. There is conditional replacement interference and severe truthful collateral.

**Not established:** pure-pressure encoding, natural routing, unique L17H24 key mediation (group6 serves heads24–27), complete pathways, mediated fractions, natural competition/backups, selective suppression, or intent. Donor-content changes remain; frozen direct paths and freely evolving family replacement are different estimands. Exploratory pointwise story-bootstrap intervals and selected native confirmation constrain strength and scope.

## Minimal claim-level uncertainties

1. **Behavioral variable:** Which independently manipulated cue predicts selection, and is any eventual consequence phenotype distinct from neutral task/default errors? The frozen neutral cue panel addresses only part of this; it cannot establish consequence sensitivity.
2. **Computational variable:** Do implicated interfaces carry reference/content or a consequence-specific selection change? Current intervention effects do not discriminate these meanings.
3. **Causal explanation and scope:** Can one account predict held-out behavior/intervention outcomes while distinguishing shared task damage and donor replacement, with factual collateral measured? Selectivity and transfer remain unshown; broad publication claims require evidence commensurate with their scope.

Await the single frozen cue panel. Its eight reused fixtures, lexical/example alias, naming/salience changes and bundled task stems limit interpretation even if rule predictions separate. No result can revive failed gates or automatically authorize a causal grid. Do not expand tools or semantic head labels before resolving what behavior the proposed mechanism explains.

## High-level summary

The project has credible conditional QK-path evidence and informative negative validation results, but no identified pressure-specific deception mechanism. Shared membership does not make a head generic; exclusive membership does not make it deception-specific. Native D−F is strong, while its external evidence is weak. Coupled-first selection and task-dependent retrieval make assay sensitivity a serious alternative, not a proven explanation of overlap or transfer. The frozen cue probe can distinguish some reference-selection predictions; it cannot establish consequence sensitivity, intent or a complete circuit. The immediate bottleneck is identifying the behavioral variable and its causal interpretation, rather than adding tools.
