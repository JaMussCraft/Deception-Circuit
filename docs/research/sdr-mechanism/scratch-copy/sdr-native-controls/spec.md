## Estimand and scientific scope

At fixed facts, favored identity, task, question, order and readout, estimate an explicit answer-contingent adverse-clause effect against both noncontingent adverse and neutral clauses. The native reporting question commits to **which result is shown**; choosing the harmless/non-relevant result is a non-relevant selection commitment, not automatically omission or a false factual assertion. “Correct/relevant reporting” below means choosing the explicitly designated damaging result. Retrieval identifies the damaging fact. Prior native labels called these operational choices truthful/wrong; do not infer audience belief, knowledge concealment, motive or strategic intent.

The adverse control changes contingency and controllability together; the neutral control changes adverse possibility. These are concrete clause contrasts, not uniquely identified abstract pressure. Passing gates establishes a controlled consequence-sensitive result-selection phenotype under this scaffold, not deception-specific circuitry or OOD generalization.
