# Independent priority critique: original SDR masks

2026-10-25. Read root AGENTS.md, shared CLAUDE.md, relevant shared memory, the completed mechanism status, cue-circuit transplant findings, original mechanism review, cue-circuit specification and technical analysis. No new literature search, model execution, code changes or git operation. This review concerns original masks only; separate v2 work and all failed behavioral gates remain outside its scope.

## Verdict

**A small complementary position experiment deserves implementation now as one bounded diagnostic. Prefer the 4,224-completion early/tail 2×2 to the proposed 5,504-completion three-single-block scan.** Its scientific contribution is an operational decomposition of an already established replacement effect, not a mechanism of deception. It should not automatically authorize another neutral localization, head search or pathway grid. The next major scientific checkpoint must assess whether there is a viable behaviorally grounded route back to incentive-conditioned reporting; continuing to explain the neutral scaffold indefinitely would miss the research objective.

The original completed study is stronger than a generic localization result: the shared32 core transfers two independently manipulated input influences, while exclusive A22 and overlap B11 have opposite signed example-order effects. But it does not explain how consequences produce deception. Reporting remains a commitment about what to show, and the existing neutral behavior/capability failures remain failures. A successful position diagnostic cannot repair them.

## Why the three-block proposal buys relatively little

E changes two permitted-example identity tokens in USER. T changes damaging/harmless facts in SYSTEM. Consequently, with aligned causal attention, SYSTEM-only E replacement is a structural no-op: those donor states cannot depend on later changed USER tokens. T can influence later system tokens, user tokens and the supplied assistant prefix. E can influence user tokens after the changed example and the supplied assistant prefix. A three-role result may largely rediscover this imposed causal geometry.

Assistant-prefix effects are particularly easy to overinterpret. Those tokens are downstream of the entire prompt, so their donor activations can carry a compiled answer, a contextual preference, record content or several of these. Strong assistant-only transfer would establish that fixed donor states can inject an influence there. It would not locate where that influence was first computed, identify its representation, or show a selector naturally mediates behavior.

Three singleton blocks plus full-prefix replay also omit the complementary conditions required to ask whether one block's effect depends on replacement in the others. Enlarging that design to three singles, three complements and full produces 9,344 completions. There is no current scientific reason to pay for that finer partition before knowing whether the broad source-versus-tail distinction is informative.

## Minimal design worth implementing

Freeze two disjoint exhaustive token sets per original prompt:

- **Early:** BOS, system and user content and intervening template tokens, ending immediately before the first token of the supplied assistant header.
- **Tail:** the complete supplied assistant header and answer stem through the final original-prefix token. Generated tokens are always excluded.

For each original directed donor pair and each of S32 and the same fixed C_S outside comparator, run early-only, tail-only and their full-prefix union. The shared intact recipient baseline is the neither condition. Retain all original 384 prompts and 640 E/report, T/report and T/retrieval pairs: 384 + 2×640×3 = **4,224 scientific completions**. Use the original sustained prefix replacement, original full-answer decoding, all natural MLPs, original immutable panel and fixed comparator. Replaying full is needed to bind the changed implementation to the completed effect, rather than treating an earlier run as an interchangeable factorial cell.

For each fixed semantic outcome indicator Y, report all four cells and the interaction

`Y(full) - Y(early) - Y(tail) + Y(intact)`.

Also report the simple conditional differences: tail minus intact versus full minus early; early minus intact versus full minus tail. Preserve per-fixture counts, reciprocal directions, input-defined strata, contradictions, refusal and other noncommitments. Keep actual intact-recipient/donor/patched answer transitions visible. Do not change denominators to successful or discordant answers. One fixed C_S remains a comparator, not a random-null distribution or an importance-matched specificity test.

This design tests a small, concrete distinction: can the established donor influence enter through pre-assistant positions, through the supplied assistant prefix, or through conditional contributions of both? Both selective tail sufficiency and a stronger early contribution are informative operational results. If both partial arms are weak and only full is strong, the proper conclusion is dependence on the joint replacement/background, not discovery of a distributed natural mechanism. Opposite signs or endpoint interactions do not by themselves establish redundancy, competition or compensatory pathways.

## Boundary mechanics are release conditions

The partition must derive from exact CHAT_SHELL bytes, token IDs and tokenizer offsets, including special tokens. Assert every valid original token belongs to exactly one arm, their intersection is empty, their union equals the original prefix mask, donor/recipient boundaries agree, and padding/generated positions never enter either arm. Do not infer the boundary by finding the word “assistant” or by classifying every shell token as early; shell tokens also occur at the assistant boundary. A token spanning a proposed character boundary needs an explicit frozen assignment or a failed audit, not a silent offset approximation.

Independently check the full-prefix state geometry: before the first changed input token, donor and recipient causal states must agree on the same numerical path. E's system-side prefix is an especially useful exact no-op check. This belongs in numerical calibration/control receipts and does not require adding an entire main scientific arm. Self, empty-map, repeat and sustained-step controls should cover both position maps and their union; replay must preserve the original arm semantics across generation. These requirements are directly about whether the intervention means what its label says.

## What stronger alternatives would and would not buy

**Joint E×T counterfactuals:** These are potentially more semantic than role localization, but reversing both inputs and patching the whole prefix does not discriminate factor transport from compiled-answer transport. A worthwhile future factorial would freeze a high-level model and its predicted outcome for crossed or mixed donor states at one selected locus, then test previously unseen combinations. Using different donors for different source blocks can create internally inconsistent factual histories; each donor state's upstream information must be specified. This is a separate experiment, not an extra axis to append without a discriminating prediction. Existing same-emitted-answer counterexamples reject universal copying of that emitted answer, not every possible compiled-choice representation.

**Head-group decomposition:** The opposite signed A22/B11 effects make this a better candidate than ranking individual heads by knockout. It still does not answer the deception question on the neutral panel. Adding A/B/J to this position study multiplies cells without resolving the central missing causal variable. Defer it unless the position result and a prospective model identify a specific unseen intervention it can predict.

**Return to stronger native behavior:** This has the highest research value and the highest risk of silently reviving a failed protocol. Strong native two-choice performance alone is insufficient when example identity, requested format and semantic reporting are confounded. A later design must establish a new, explicit estimand and viable pressure/content/answer contrasts using the original masks, preserve failed-gate records and distinguish benchmark answer production from false assertion. Existing artifacts can be audited without running a model. It is legitimate for that audit to conclude no currently supported matched contrast exists. It is not legitimate to relabel a failed capability or neutral-reporting gate as passed because this new localization looks clean.

## Claim and continuation limits

No possible outcome of the recommended diagnostic establishes pressure mediation, intent, audience modeling, reporting-time latent knowledge, content-preserving suppression, natural redundancy, complete pathways, clean task selectivity or OOD transfer. Neither E/T amplitude nor early/tail amplitude ranks what a head encodes. Full-head replacements across multiple layers remain conditional interventions that can overwrite intermediate natural computation.

The result can narrow one implementation-level causal ambiguity and help choose a future test. It cannot by itself support a publishable deception mechanism. The publication criterion from the original review still applies: specify a computation that predicts held-out interventions better than shared task processing and donor overwrite. If this diagnostic only shows broad downstream transport, finish the result concisely and return to behavioral identifiability instead of launching a finer neutral scan.
