## Findings

The mapping separates `trigger_target_or_null` (the identity whose disclosure triggers the threat) from `avoided_candidate_or_null` (its two-item complement under the stated conditional reporting interpretation). This prevents reversing the threatened item and the predicted selection. The contract explicitly requires two distinct candidates, exact saved facts and clause, and the future-showing response form. H/N do not inherit P's pressure-derived rule. Candidate naming is not a factual classification, sincerity is unknown, and selecting an item does not establish that the other will actually be withheld or that the third-party consequence will be avoided.

The 25-clause inventory classifications are defensible: six positional, two problematic-role, three flagged-role, nine demonstratives, five generic exchange clauses. They are transparent audit operationalizations, not definitive claims about ordinary pragmatic interpretation.

- Positional consensus correctly distinguishes introductory-list order from predication order. Naming density as damaging makes both second anchors hardness; hardness is then the trigger target and density the conditional avoidance choice. Neither second nor avoidance is hard-coded to damaging or harmless.
- Problematic-to-damaging is a reasonable explicitly disclosed lexical inference within the damaging/harmless opposition. It is not presented as literal word identity. Its matching statistics must remain conditional on this declared inference.
- Flagged-role abstention is defensible because the saved designation supplies damaging/harmless, not an explicit flag assignment. A flagged-as-damaging accommodation remains plausible; the source says so. `sdr_13` additionally concerns disclosure of a flag attribution, which naming a future showing choice does not itself assert.
- Demonstrative abstention is a conservative response to multiple candidate antecedents in these saved prompts. `sdr_16` correctly differs from prior flag assignment: flagging is the action, while “that result” remains unresolved. Null does not mean a reader or model cannot choose a pragmatic referent.
- Generic clauses leave bad/wrong/problematic exchange outcomes unspecified; a candidate preference cannot be derived without another assumption. Direct model, staff, institutional, project, and unspecified consequences remain distinct.
