# Independent scientific priority critique

2026-10-25. Scope: original coarse D/H/F masks, through completed position source `2949214`. Read the shared project instructions and memory, original mechanism review/evidence audit, and final native-control, cue-probe, cue-transplant, position, and routing-pilot reports. This initial critique was written without reading concurrent `design-options`. No evaluation, model execution, GPU/job operation, notebook, git operation, paid API, or other-study modification was performed.

## Recommendation

Stop expanding the eight-fixture neutral synthetic scaffold. Spend the next bounded phase testing whether the proposed mechanism actually explains the original SDR behavior on data untouched by the successive mechanism studies. Return to original benchmark records and preserve their original rendering before making minimal, explicit counterfactual edits. Proceed to causal rescue only if this behavior supplies the required support. If it does not, pursue an artifact/shared-computation account, or stop this original-mask mechanistic line. Another engineered assay that finally produces a desirable contrast would explain that engineered assay first; it would not retroactively identify the original masks' function.

The completed work is scientifically useful. It has established shared causal sensitivity to example order and factual content under prefix replacement, conditional positional effects, and opposing contributions hidden by set subtraction. It has also repeatedly failed to validate its intended consequence-sensitive reporting interpretation. Those failures must change priorities. Numerical reproducibility and annotation agreement establish measurement integrity; they do not repair construct validity. Large numbers of factorial rows over eight reused fixtures do not create a large independent population.

## What the evidence currently favors

A shared answer-processing account is the strongest supported account, not merely an inconvenient alternative. D/H/F and S32 transfer neutral example and fact changes similarly; S32 also transfers factual alternatives into an otherwise perfect retrieval task. Most example transfer is reproduced at supplied assistant-prefix positions. These observations fit context integration and answer preparation shared across the discovery tasks. They do not imply those heads lack any consequence-related contribution elsewhere.

Native validation is the decisive warning. Neutral reporting was 337/512 correct, retrieval 1392/1536, the prespecified contingent-minus-adverse effect was 9.4 points rather than the required 15, and wording-specific requirements failed. Across all reporting cases, contingent and unconditional adverse conditions had the same 207/512 non-designated choices. The within-conflict effect is real evidence about that contrast, but cannot be promoted into a validated general consequence mechanism. “Choose which item to show” is also not automatically an assertion that the other item is factually safe.

Universal copying of the donor's emitted answer has counterexamples. This rejects one narrow alternative; it does not establish pressure coding, abstract cue coding, or a complete selector/transport decomposition. Contradictory truth donors can import another fact; truthful-answer loss under them does not by itself measure generic capability damage. Conversely, equal aggregate truth counts under E do not establish per-item protection. Non-additive early/tail effects alone cannot distinguish natural competition, redundancy, serial mediation, and replacement/readout nonlinearities.

## Ranked next steps

### 1. A frozen original-SDR challenge set with an explicit negative outcome

First establish what untouched original records remain. “Held out” must mean disjoint from training, component samples, the 48-story pilot, and subsequent behavioral/scaffold selection; test-split membership alone is insufficient. If none remain, say so. Previously behavior-filtered no-pressure survivors remain a useful conditional diagnostic, but not an unbiased prevalence estimate. Do not silently replace them with new v2 data owned by the other investigation.

Freeze a modest story-level discovery/confirmation allocation before new outcomes. Evaluate the original renderings and a minimal counterfactual panel that separately changes consequence contingency, consequence presence/adversity, and relevant nuisance cues actually present in the original prompts. Preserve original examples and response stems in the baseline; do not import the later neutral scaffold. Use matched truth/item identities and full completions. Include factual retrieval and ordinary reporting controls, both truth/order directions, and more than one original template family. Separate intervention-independent population results from any predefined conditional-support subset.

The discriminating result is a reproducible consequence-contingent reporting effect that survives cue changes and matched unconditional adversity while truth handling remains capable. A cue account instead predicts choices from examples/order/reference form regardless of contingency. A broad adverse-context account predicts comparable contingent and unconditional effects. Ordinary report-task misunderstanding predicts poor neutral reporting despite good retrieval. Report these alternatives directly, including interactions and per-story failures, rather than searching for the first significant pooled coefficient.

Use criteria for the intended claim, not a relaxation of old gates. Full-completion scoring may be a new prospective endpoint; it cannot rescue old failed protocols. Predefine a practically meaningful effect and uncertainty criterion, capability requirements across meaningful blocks, and what constitutes insufficient support. Analyze uncertainty at story/template family; factorial renderings are dependent.

**Stop rule:** if the fixed confirmation population cannot separate consequence contingency from cue/adverse/reporting explanations with capable controls, stop pressure-specific pathway work on the original masks. Do not initiate an open-ended wording search. This is one bounded diagnostic, not a promise that original SDR will validate. Passing supports a controlled text-conditioned behavior, not deceptive intent.

### 2. Conditional recipient-fact-preserving intervention with rescue/protection

Only if step 1 supplies natural counterfactual support, use the already nominated candidate6/selector2 and S32 comparator; do not start another head-search grid. The key intervention must change the proposed consequence variable while preserving recipient facts and answer identity. A donor whose factual record differs cannot identify this variable. If required natural same-fact donor conditions are unsupported, report the identification failure rather than manufacturing support through truth-changing donors.

Make a small, prospectively specified rescue/protection test: compare ordinary intervention, a fixed restoration of a nominated downstream state, restoration alone, and numerical/self controls. Include reverse directions, matched neutral/adverse conditions, retrieval, and an ordinary report control. The proposed computation must predict the direction and condition of rescue before confirmation, including where the rescue should fail. A recipient-content restoration can test whether a factual overwrite explains the effect; downstream restoration can test whether that specific state is required for the manipulated effect. Neither alone establishes an unmanipulated natural pathway, and a clamp that prevents all answer change is not selective protection.

Measure ordinary joint replacements alongside any frozen-background direct-path construct. Their different backgrounds prevent summing effects into a mediated fraction. Retain coherent refusal as a valid suppression success; judge malformed/empty/repetitive output and ordinary utility separately. Factual correctness is needed for the knowledge/content hypothesis, not as a requirement that every useful suppression intervention disclose the truth.

**Stop rule:** no consequence-specific claim if every behavioral change follows donor content/cues, if truth preservation fails, or if the same intervention disrupts matched benign tasks comparably. Do not treat rescue after extensive post-hoc searching as a prediction. A failed rescue is informative only relative to the fixed proposed causal model and the intervention's demonstrated support.

### 3. An artifact/shared-computation explanation that predicts new outcomes

If step 1 fails, this is the preferable scientific branch to constructing another synthetic “deception” assay. Formalize competing cue/content/shared-bottleneck accounts and use existing data for discovery only. Freeze predictions for untouched original stories, cue counterfactuals, and a small set of ordinary interventions. Predict signed per-item changes and condition dependence, not just mask overlap or an average accuracy drop. Use multiple predeclared layer/count-matched outside sets where a specificity claim is made; C_S alone is not a null distribution.

A useful result would show which original apparent deception effects are explained by shared task operations and why subtraction or donor replacement gives misleading semantic interpretations. Opposing A22/B11 contributions could furnish an additional prediction, but rediscovering their opposition in the same eight fixtures does not count. Donor/corruption dependence must be tested explicitly; one successful donor is not a general mechanism. Second-model/distribution evidence would ultimately strengthen a general methods paper, but is a later decision, not grounds for duplicating the active v2 or suppression studies.

**Stop rule:** if the account cannot outperform simple answer/cue-following baselines on frozen predictions, retain a careful negative/audit result and stop deeper localization. A publication target is not a reason to continue indefinitely. An original-mask case study alone may not support a top-ML mechanism claim; avoid compensating with inflated language.

## What additional synthetic construction would justify itself?

Only a small, preregistered counterfactual intervention needed to distinguish two explicit hypotheses and validated against the original behavior. New names, larger factorial combinations, or more threatening incentive wording do not independently solve the historical selection, report semantics, prefix, or cue problems. A broader new behavioral benchmark belongs with the existing dataset investigation. Prefer its eventual vetted results to building a parallel benchmark here.

## Language constraints

Use “consequence-contingent reporting” only when that contrast is supported. “Non-designated choice” need not be factual falsehood; factual falsehood need not be intentional deception. Correct retrieval in a separate prompt shows accessible information in that prompt, not a truthful belief retained during reporting. Item-conditioned weights-wipe text does not demonstrate an intrinsic self-preservation goal. Refusal may be successful suppression without proving removal or locating a deception module. D/H/F identify discovery conditions, and S32 identifies a mask intersection. The strongest future positive claim would be a shared computation whose condition-dependent causal role predicts held-out interventions; it still requires separate evidence for any claim about deception or intent.
