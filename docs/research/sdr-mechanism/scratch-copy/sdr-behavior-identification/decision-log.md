# Decisions for the original-native evidence audit

1. Reconnect with the original SDR phenotype before expanding neutral cue localization. The previous neutral position findings remain useful conditional replacement evidence; they do not determine the native phenotype.
2. Complete a finite retrospective CPU analysis of archived data. Do not spend GPU time generating another synthetic capability panel until an actual discriminating contrast is identified.
3. Analyze native first-token and native full-completion evidence separately. Historical selection, prior inspection, clause differences and missing cells remain visible. No new success threshold can turn an older failed protocol into a pass.
4. Use a transparent source-text semantic appendix and independent review, not a claim of blinded response scoring. Existing full-response labels remain unchanged.
5. Keep analysis artifacts under this tracking directory. No original source edits, new infrastructure, v2 evaluation, training, paid judge, merge/push, job submission or Bridges-2 access belongs to this audit.

These are lead decisions under the user's standing delegation, not additional user-specified scientific hypotheses. Their main cost if wrong is opportunity cost: a small CPU/agent audit may fail to uncover a usable contrast. They do not invalidate or overwrite prior results.
