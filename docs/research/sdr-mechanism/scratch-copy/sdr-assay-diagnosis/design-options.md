# Next-step design critique after native-controls failure

2026-10-21. Design proposal only; no experiments, source changes, dataset changes, label changes or budget reservations. Lead compactions remain **4**.

**Recommendation after the completed error audit: one small behavioral cue-decoupling probe is more informative than further summaries of the confounded old panel. Stop repairing a consequence-driven assay in this investigation.** The useful remaining question is what the original D/H/F masks (69/56/68 heads) contribute to observed answer selection. Their names do not establish deception, honesty or format modules. A new rich tracing method cannot supply the missing behavioral identification.

## What changes the decision

The native study failed 30/105 fixed checks, including neutral reporting and absolute retrieval capability. Its protocol ends at a complete behavioral report on any failure; no new pathway grid follows. Its all-reporting C and A rates are identical, 207/512, although the prespecified conflicting contrast is positive. Neither fact erases the other.

The concurrent error-structure audit supplies a sharper descriptive alternative: reporting chooses the first explicit record/question option in 1,305/1,536 responses; 563/589 reporting errors arise when that option is harmless. Reversing order changes report identity in 541/768 pairs, whereas reversing truth leaves report identity unchanged in 575/768. These are post-hoc findings from the full panel. Record order, option order and explicit-versus-"other" designation are coupled, so they identify a composite reference/position hypothesis, not a unique first-position mechanism. The semantics audit finds no obvious gold-label inversion and emphasizes that report responses commit to a future selection rather than assert the wrong factual predicate.

Previous pilot perturbations already contradict universal donor-emitted-item copying, locate conditional Q/K interfaces and show reciprocal truthful-answer harm. Repeating those same donor contrasts or selecting their favorable strata adds little. Pure pressure, latent knowledge, intent and selective suppression remain unidentified.

## Three genuinely different choices

### 1. Existing-data model discrimination: low-cost alternative, limited remaining value

Question: can a simple reference/position account explain the full answer-selection pattern, and what causal facts from the original pilot remain unexplained?

Use existing native-control joins plus the original pilot Stage 1/2 saved comparisons; no new model forwards. Specify a small prediction table before further analysis: record-supported identity, first explicit referent, favored identity, and donor-emitted identity. Compare their identity predictions and disagreement cells across every existing task/condition/wording/truth/order block. Report abstention/non-item outcomes explicitly. Do not fit a flexible classifier that can memorize factor cells, and do not use the original pilot's already-inspected confirmation split as fresh confirmation.

For existing patch results, retain all available comparisons, both reciprocal directions, both scopes and every frozen path. Orient margin changes separately toward the recipient record-supported answer, explicit referent and donor emitted answer. Tabulate prediction failures and missing-support cells rather than interpreting every nonzero effect as routing. Existing same-false-donor findings already reject a universal copying account; the added value must be a full-panel predictive decomposition, not another report of that rejection. Keep unsupported proposed contrasts visibly unsupported.

Falsifiable hypothesis: a reference/position rule predicts reporting substantially better than a record-supported rule across the existing panel; record-supported identity predicts retrieval better. A stronger mechanistic hypothesis would additionally predict which frozen family/path perturbations move outputs toward the donor's answer reference. The latter can be rejected even if the behavioral rule succeeds. Directional exceptions and H/N harm are required evidence, not noise to filter.

Scientific scope: an exploratory account of task-dependent answer selection and the causal sensitivity of original masks. Associations between existing behavioral factors and patch effects cannot identify a natural mediator. Native controls and original pilot have different scaffolds; transfer between them is a hypothesis, not an automatic join.

Cost: CPU-only, zero API/GPU. Stop after one bounded analysis and an explicit prediction-failure table. If all remaining hypotheses make the same predictions on available support, state non-identifiability and stop. Do not mine new paths, thresholds, stories or masks until one explanation appears to win.

### 2. One orthogonal reference probe: recommended next design, behavioral only

Question: is the observed report bias tied to first explicit mention, question-option order, or the relationship expressed by the record?

This needs a genuinely new contrast because existing factors are coupled. Preserve the original neutral N clause, favored cue, norm, assistant stems, lexical items and historical suffix punctuation. Independently cross first record item, question-option order and record-supported identity. Add original "other" records versus minimally explicit named records as a factor. Include reporting and factual retrieval. Fix permitted-output examples in lexical order across ALL record/question cells; preserving their original record-dependent order would alias record-following with example-following. Reverse only the interrogative options for question order. This fixed-example convention is an additional explicit scaffold change, not an unchanged original baseline. Do not change the retrieval prefill in this probe. This is a new reference-selection diagnostic derived from original records, not a repaired deception assay.

Compact proposed behavioral pilot: 8 fixtures chosen by frozen execution rank, four per native template, never baseline success; 2 record forms ×2 record orders ×2 question orders ×2 truth identities ×2 favored identities ×2 tasks =512 planned completions. One frozen wording per task, semantic scoring over complete responses, all planned rows retained. One round only. Prediction: a mention-position mechanism follows the first record item when question order changes; a question-order mechanism follows the question's first candidate; record-based computation tracks the supported identity across both. An anaphora/explicitness account predicts a shift specifically when the second item is named, especially when the damaging result occupies the second record position. Favor-following predicts favored identity across independent order and truth changes. Qualified/ambiguous answers remain unresolved rather than forced into a candidate. Report discordant-factor cells rather than pooled accuracy alone.

For example, replace only "The stocks result is the one that is harmless, and the other is damaging" with "The stocks result is the one that is harmless, and the bonds result is damaging". A fully symmetric alternative ("The stocks result is harmless. The bonds result is damaging.") is semantically valid but also changes conjunction and first-clause syntax; choose the minimal substitution for this first probe. Even that substitution changes token content, length, repetition and salience, so the contrast is an explicit-reference intervention rather than proof of a uniquely anaphoric computation. The question swap changes only "stocks or bonds" to "bonds or stocks" in the question; do not accidentally reverse the lexically fixed permitted-output examples too. Task still bundles question/stem/response-format differences; no pure task-format effect follows. Eight fixtures preserve both templates but do not identify template versus lexical effects.

Only if these predictions separate on the whole panel would a separate reviewed intervention proposal target original candidate6 and selector2 plus matched outside-head controls. The first causal comparison should be ordinary family replacement or a component-output test with canonical identity mapping, not a Q/K/V grid. K-group6 is shared by heads24–27; a group effect cannot be labeled L17H24-specific. Any test must compare predictions against donor overwrite and ordinary factual collateral, retain all support and preserve all MLPs explicitly in the scope statement.

Cost method, not a reservation: dry-run exact rendering/lengths; calibrate the actual model/device/batch/length with full controls and worst-case completion cap; project all512 rows and any separately frozen intervention rows, adding measured load/I/O/shutdown overhead. Do not extrapolate main runtime from short average answers or reuse a different GPU's calibration. Prefer one ≤10-minute calibration and one ≤30-minute single-GPU behavioral allocation only if its measured worst-case forecast fits; otherwise revise the proposed operational envelope before running, not the scientific panel after outcomes. No causal allocation is justified yet.

Freeze the prediction table, exact renderer, fixture IDs and scoring rubric before outputs. There is no new accuracy/effect threshold whose passage could rescue the failed assay. Stop after this one factorial panel. If orthogonal rendering is semantically disputed or outputs cannot distinguish the candidate rules, report that limit; do not replace fixtures, retune wording or select a successful subset. Even a clear result establishes a narrow reference-selection computation, not consequence sensitivity or deception.

This option is preferable to option1 now because the completed error audit already establishes the composite default and only new orthogonal variation can distinguish its causes. Its distinction from concurrent v2 scaffold repair is the frozen original-record task, a single cue factorial, no threat retuning, no search for deception elicitation and no v2 masks or evaluations. The failed native protocol itself authorizes no such extension; a separate explicit scope is necessary, and this document does not release execution.

A head-family follow-up fits the original D/H/F goal only conditionally. Freeze a small intervention proposal after behavior identifies which predictions differ, then test original candidate6/selector2 families and outside-head controls on all preselected discordant factor cells plus matched agreeing cells, not successful baselines. The target would be causal sensitivity to the identified reference variable while measuring record-answer collateral; it would not establish that every head in D/H/F has that role. A failure to separate variables at the behavioral stage ends this branch. Do not automatically revive the old Q/K grid or expand to all69/56/68 individual heads.

### 3. Defer new circuit work to the existing v2 review gate

The concurrent pipeline already spent multiple rounds on elicitation/scaffold repair, built all FAR/SDR/SER v2 datasets and obtained user G12 approval for six coarse heads-only D/H training runs. A second three-mechanism reconstruction or another pressure-wording search here duplicates that work. The v2 construction-based purity decision and recorded judge disagreement also do not license stronger psychological labels.

Read-only state inspection: RESUME.md still says training waits, but later spec§14.G12 and the live 05-handback record coarse-only training approval/submissions. I did not query or alter jobs and do not assert their current completion. All sources retain an explicit hard stop before circuit evaluations, faithfulness, knockouts, compositions or EVAL5 until user review. This continuation does not override that gate. Fine training is separately deferred.

This option costs no new compute here. It offers a distinct opportunity to test scaffold-matched contrasts later, conditional on the separate user's review. It cannot retrospectively validate the original masks or rescue the native-controls gate. Waiting for that review is appropriate if the user needs a defensible mechanism comparison rather than a descriptive mechanism of the original model's answer bias.

## Decision and evidence boundary

Choose option2 as the smallest informative new design if pursuing the original answer-selection mechanism now; keep it behavioral-only and one round. Option1 remains a zero-compute alternative but cannot resolve coupled variables, and much of its behavioral value is already supplied by the completed audit. Option3 is the route for broader task-mechanism comparison and retains its own user gate. Keep the original conditional QK pathways as hypotheses and preserve every failed validation verdict.
