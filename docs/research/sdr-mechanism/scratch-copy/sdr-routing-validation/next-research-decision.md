# Next research decision after the completion diagnostic

2026-10-21. Decision memo only; no new implementation or experiments authorized here. Original confirmatory validation remains closed at its failed first-token validity gate, and Task 2 remains stopped. Sources: [baseline analysis](baseline-analysis.md), [completion diagnostic spec](completion-diagnostic-spec.md), and [completed native pilot report](../../project_context/reports/SDR%20routing%20pilot%20findings.md).

**Recommendation: prioritize a prospectively scored native matched-control study over repairing the bonus-contingency assay.** Use the completion diagnostic to diagnose output format and characterize behavior, not to choose favorable rows or revive the old path grid. The strongest next mechanistic target is whether shared D/H/F components transport factual-answer information and consequence-context information differently under reporting versus factual retrieval. “A deception-specific circuit” is currently a substantially stronger claim than the evidence supports.

## What is already informative

Numerical controls passed, but template A neutral retrieval failed its frozen full-vocabulary first-token gate. All 512 fresh retrieval rows nevertheless rank the true candidate above the other candidate; this motivates completion, not retrospective capability certification. Fresh reporting already follows favored identity under fixed bonuses, while conditional-bonus changes do not systematically lower truth margins. Thus resolving prefix errors alone would not establish the intended consequence-driven phenotype.

The native bridge has a much larger clause-dependent contrast: truthful item argmax declines from 101/128 without pressure to 34/128 with pressure. However, it is historically behaviorally filtered and strongly truth/order dependent. The completed pilot locates conditional perturbation interfaces and shows honest collateral; it does not establish a pure pressure variable, a complete causal graph, or selective suppression.

## Decisions conditional on complete responses

| Completion finding, retaining all frozen rows | Scientific implication | Next decision |
|---|---|---|
| Retrieval is overwhelmingly correct after prefixes; reporting follows favored identity similarly under fixed and conditional bonus | Much first-token failure is formatting; reporting differences remain consistent with task/goal following | Do not salvage bonus as the primary pressure assay. Retain it as an exploratory benign goal-context comparison; prioritize native matched controls |
| Retrieval is correct, and full reporting reveals a substantial within-story conditional-bonus effect absent in first-token outcomes | A potentially useful behavioral readout was missed, but the observation is post hoc and may reflect completion wording | Permit one prospectively specified replication on new complete fixtures with frozen full-response scoring before any causal grid. No selected-template rescue |
| Retrieval often ends wrong, contradictory, or ambiguous | The proposed factual control genuinely lacks reliable completion competence under this scaffold | No pathway interpretation of this assay. Return to native controls with a prospectively validated readout; do not repeatedly tune bonus prompts |
| Many outputs cap, fail checksum, or remain unclassifiable | Completion diagnostic cannot decide the intended comparison | Report unresolved denominators. No inference from apparent early answers and no selected-row extensions; redesign only under a separate protocol |

“Substantial” must become a prospectively chosen practically meaningful effect and uncertainty criterion before any replication. Do not select that threshold from these completions. Neither universal truthful reporting nor a null stakes effect is a reason for adaptive pressure search.

## Ranked next research directions

**1. Establish the native counterfactual phenotype with stronger matched controls.** Hold record, named identities, favored/report target, question and readout fixed while changing only a specified consequence clause. Include a noncontingent adverse-event/context control and benign factual retrieval, not only pressure deletion or an added honesty instruction. Cross truth and item order and keep instruction-H separate from no-pressure-N. Freeze an unselected story panel and full-response scoring; report the historical filtered bridge separately. This tests the effect of an explicit clause manipulation, not a uniquely isolated abstract pressure state.

**Go:** factual retrieval and neutral reporting satisfy prospective whole-panel capability criteria, and the consequence contrast reproduces across truth/order cells and wording blocks without survivor selection. **No-go:** effects depend on one polarity/position, factual performance collapses, or a nuisance adverse-event control explains the contrast. In that case the publishable result is scaffold-sensitive reporting/selection, not strategic deception.

**2. Test conditional reuse within shared D/H/F machinery, only after the phenotype is established.** Reuse frozen candidate routes, matched stakes-only versus truth-only donors, identical semantic-position support, reciprocal transfers, matched outside-mask controls and an ordinary free-evolving family replacement. Include a limited full-model/MLP dependency check. Add prospective family-level uncertainty and a held-out story split. No speculative semantic head names are needed.

**Go:** a context-only donor changes the predicted reporting outcome at fixed facts, the same route's content intervention behaves as predicted, and task/control contrasts replicate without broad factual damage. **No-go:** only changed-truth donors work, donor-answer identity predicts everything, or numerical/mapping/control effects dominate. A frozen direct path demonstrates a perturbation route under its background; it is not the fraction of natural computation mediated or a complete causal graph. Necessity in the intact model and sufficiency with all MLPs retained remain separate tests.

**3. Keep the fresh bonus task as a secondary goal-processing assay.** Even excellent completed retrieval would not erase the strong fixed-bonus favored-context effect. A new prospective full-response replication is worthwhile only if it tests a specific discriminating prediction, not simply whether a different phrasing elicits more wrong answers. Task/readout following and adapting to a sponsor's stated preference are behaviorally different from knowingly inducing another agent's false belief for a strategic objective.

**4. Defer feature dictionaries and large discovery runs.** SAE/transcoder or subspace analysis becomes useful when a concrete variable and causal comparison survive the preceding tests. Better coordinates cannot repair a missing behavioral contrast or confounded donor.

## Strongest defensible claim to target

**“Consequence context conditionally changes how shared factual-answer pathways are used during constrained reporting.”** This is falsifiable by matched no-pressure/adverse-context/retrieval controls and content-only donors. Establishing it would explain why D/H/F overlap can matter more than difference masks without treating overlap as a semantic role. A component becomes *critical* only under a stated necessity/background test; membership or large replacement effects alone are insufficient. Strategic deception, selective suppression and external generalization would require additional evidence beyond this claim.
