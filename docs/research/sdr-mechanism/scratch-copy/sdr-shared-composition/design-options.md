# SDR shared composition: proposed causal explanations

2026-10-26. Design-only contribution. Root has accepted the bounded design direction; final protocol decisions and independent critique remain pending. This document creates no run authorization or reservation. Original native reports/results remain immutable; stopped named-consequence 3,840-head assay remains stopped.

The scientific target is how reused components combine to change the result the model commits to showing in original SDR. Shared use in ordinary tasks is expected and informative. The bounded study below asks whether an early candidate perturbation needs an induced L17 attention state or output to change the commitment, and whether the same induced state can transfer that change without the upstream perturbation. It does not require a new prompt scaffold, exclusive deception heads, selective suppression, or pristine behavior.

## What the archive already answers

Candidate6 is L12H6, L13H5/H20/H21, L14H24, L17H26. Selector2 is L17H24/L24H27. Every candidate and selector belongs to shared32. A candidate/shared32 joint intervention would therefore be a union containing the candidate, not an independent two-family factorial.

Archived Stage2 froze every component except one sender in pass1, captured receiver Q/K/V before RoPE, then inserted that receiver into a recipient with freely evolving downstream computation. Its actual primary receiver replacement covered **all recipient prefix positions**; the preparatory narrower receiver-mask function was not called. The largest confirmed direct K routes occur with fact-changing donors. For native fixed-fact clause-only P←N, confirmation K paths are close to zero and none of the tested single paths changes argmax, while ordinary candidate6 changes 11/24 choices. Do not describe those old K routes as pressure mediation.

The new experiment changes the counterfactual: capture the receiver after an ordinary **freely evolving** multi-sender intervention; then restore a receiver to its unperturbed recipient state while that intervention runs, or transfer the induced receiver state alone. This tests dependence and conditional sufficiency of the induced state under replacement. It includes indirect and MLP routes and is not another isolated direct-edge measurement.

## Three different explanations and readable predictions

| Explanation | What the parts do together | New result that would support it | Result that weakens it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Context-dependent selection | Earlier candidate5 alters which contextual evidence a reused L17 attention computation selects. Shared32 supplies ordinary content and context processing. | In fixed-fact P↔N, U5 changes completed commitments and induced receiver states; recipient Q/K restoration reduces that change, joint QK restoration reduces it more than either alone, and naturally induced QK alone transfers some of the same change. V restoration is smaller within matched pairs. | A sufficiently measured U5 effect survives Q/K and quartet-output restoration, or occurs only for fact changes. |
| Carried item/content | Candidate/shared state mainly transports a concrete item-related state; selection-language overinterprets content-sensitive direct paths. | Effects are larger or qualitatively different in P and N factual flips; V restoration or output restoration blocks them more than Q/K restoration; induced output transfers the literal identity without requiring the Q/K changes to reproduce the effect. S32 acts similarly in ordinary N reporting. | A reproducible fixed-fact clause effect has QK-specific blocking and rescue without corresponding factual overwrite. |
| Candidate composition within the L17 group | Earlier5 and same-layer H26 interact at or after L17; treating candidate6 as one upstream sender hides that composition. The named H24 head may be only part of a shared-GQA group. | U5 and H26 singles differ from their union U6; U6−U5−H26+baseline is nonzero in raw behavioral indicators/margins. H24-output restoration fails where restoring outputs24–27 reduces the U5 effect; induced quartet output transfers the change. | U5 already reproduces U6 and H26 adds little, while H24 alone reproduces quartet blocking. |

These explanations can coexist. Q/K versus V magnitudes alone do not rank semantic encoding. A context-related Q/K change can select content, and a content change can alter attention. The joint evidence must include native fixed-fact contrasts, full responses, recipient-state blocking, and induced-state transfer. Root explicitly defers actual attention tracing: the existing helper changes backend and lacks replacement support. Save only compact B_A−B_0 receiver norms from banks already required by the causal panel, with no extra captures or semantic interpretation. No single attention plot or logit attribution identifies a psychological role.

L24H27 remains a plausible later reader of these shared states, but this bounded study does **not** intervene on L24. Its exact downstream role remains open. Adding late L24 clamps would dilute the primary comparison; it is an explicitly deferred hypothesis, not a prerequisite or an automatic next grid.

## Recommended one study

Use the 24 historically named confirmation stories from the original pilot, fixed by their existing IDs, with both factual truth variants and only exact native P and clause-deleted N. These stories are historically familiar and selected; call this a new intervention test on archived data, not fresh confirmation or a prevalence sample. Keep 96 unique baseline prompts and all eight directed pairs per story:

- P0←N0, N0←P0, P1←N1, N1←P1: contextual clause change with fixed facts.
- P0←P1, P1←P0, N0←N1, N1←N0: factual change with fixed condition.

All 192 input-defined panels remain regardless of baseline answers. Native P factual flips alias donor emitted identity with the recipient damaging identity; do not claim literal-versus-factual-role identification from P flips alone. N flips and fixed-fact pairs are mandatory comparisons. N is the ordinary reporting comparison, using the same facts, question, answer examples and assistant stem; ordinary accuracy or shared effects do not disqualify components.

Use source U5=the five candidate heads before L17, H26=L17H26, U6=their union, and S32=the immutable shared core. Donor head replacements use the original common semantic map; unmapped positions and all other components evolve freely. U5/H26/U6 singles create a genuine disjoint 2×2 source factorial. All receivers are recipient-coordinate original-prefix replacements, generated positions excluded. Receiver sites are Q17H24, K17group6, joint Q+K, V17group6, output17H24, output17H24–27, and outside K17group0. K/V group6 affects H24–27, including H26; it is not an H24-specific intervention.

Choose outside Kgroup0 by the lowest-index whole L17 KV group outside D∪H∪F, using masks only. Archived memberships verify groups0,2,3,4,5,7 qualify; group0 supplies heads0–3. This is one fixed geometric comparator, not evidence that outsiders are inert.

Exactly 20 arms per pair plus 96 baseline completions = **3,936 EOS outcomes**. All responses use original prompt bytes/stem, deterministic greedy generation, cap64 including EOS, and all-prefix intervention reapplied at every forward while generated positions remain untouched. Numerical calibration does not select on scientific effect, answer correctness or refusal. No prompt repair, donor replacement, screening or effect-based extension.

## Cost and uncertainty

The closest archived A100 position study completed 4,224 EOS outcomes in 1,109 main GPU-seconds, plus 403 calibration seconds; 4,500 seconds had been reserved. This study adds mixed receiver banks and lightweight existing-bank norm summaries, so raw row-count scaling is only orientation. A realistic initial proposal is one **600-second numerical/timing calibration**, then a frozen main allocation forecast from measured natural batch lengths plus fixed load/I/O cost and a reasonable buffer; prior natural EOS batches of roughly3–17tokens suggest a broad **8–44 minute A100** orientation before new overhead, not a measured guarantee. Preserve cap64 and resume only incomplete planned batches if operational deadline requires it. Do not reserve a days-long all-cap worst case. No paid APIs or notebooks.

Likely informative nulls: U5 may have little native clause-only effect even if U6 has a large effect; Q/K clamps may not change a clear U5 effect; EOS commitments may differ from first-token margins. Each result narrows the mechanism. None is a reason to search for a cleaner scaffold or restart the stopped probe.
