## Estimands and buildability

The proposed operation is materially different from archived Stage2. Capture B_A under U5 in a freely evolving model, then restore recipient-baseline receiver values during the same source perturbation, or insert the induced receiver bank alone. Existing masked head/Q/K/V interfaces support these operators. The implementation seam correctly rejects old `receiver_inputs`, which freezes the rest of the model, and rejects unchanged old decoders whose single-mask contracts cannot represent partial source maps plus full-prefix receiver maps.

The blocked-effect difference-in-differences, source interaction, Q/K restoration interaction and induced-state rescue are algebraically correct. Clean-alone arms provide the required background controls. These identify **conditional dependence and sufficiency of intervention-induced interfaces**. They do not identify a natural mediated fraction, and the protocol explicitly forbids that claim. Rescuing a state alone and blocking its change are complementary tests; either can succeed without the other.

Prefix-only fixed banks, full recomputation with cache disabled and false generated/padded/dummy positions give a coherent EOS intervention. Causal prefix states do not depend on later generated suffixes. Captures therefore remain valid throughout decoding when model/backend/physical geometry are held fixed. Full-response clean-only equality to baseline through EOS/cap, firststep checksum/argmax identity, actual assignment checks and tiny-model natural-propagation tests are appropriate engineering requirements. Pre-o_proj output slices and pre-RoPE Q/K are the correct existing sites. Source/receiver writes in distinct layer17 input/output locations are implementable without new hook types.

Attention tracing is now explicitly deferred. That removes the former eager-versus-SDPA/replacement-support conflict without weakening the core study. Compact B_A−B_0 norms use already required tensors and add no execution path. They verify changed states; they cannot identify information content or causal importance. Runtime remains uncertain; a measured calibration and resumable panel are appropriate, whereas historical row scaling is only orientation.

## Minor clarifications for implementation/reporting

1. Preserve termination status (EOS/cap/error) separately from any lexical commitment label where feasible; a capped response can already contain a commitment. Publish the reversible aggregation rather than dropping either fact.
2. Report signed direction-specific effects before balanced summaries. A smaller U5 effect under a clamp is not necessarily suppression of deception; the same operator can block ordinary N factual reporting.
3. Define bank norm denominators exactly (prefix token×feature coordinates, dummy/padding excluded) and retain FP32 computation. Do not compare raw norms across interfaces as an information ranking.
4. The current design-options phrase “descriptive trace” refers only to compact bank diagnostics. Future implementation/report text should not imply attention allocation was measured.

These are reporting/implementation clarifications, not blockers or requests to expand the grid. The study addresses the user's shared-component composition objective directly; ordinary reuse, refusal or failure of previous new-scaffold gates are not prerequisites or disqualifiers here. L24, semantic state decoding and end-to-end strategic deception remain explicitly outside the bounded conclusion.
