## Finding and scope

Earlier shared-head replacement influences native disclosure choice through an input-dependent combination of layer 17 interfaces. With facts fixed, P←N effects are query-sensitive and nearly unchanged by key or value restoration. Within pressured factual swaps, key restoration is consequential in addition to query restoration. Restoring the whole H24–27 output quartet constrains U5 choice effects more than restoring H24 alone. Induced interface transfers provide incomplete conditional sufficiency: notably QK changes the clause comparison's first-step margin without changing its full-response choices.

These are intervention-relative computational constraints in an otherwise naturally evolving model. They do not identify semantic encodings, actual attention allocation, a natural serial decomposition or mediated fraction. Ordinary N effects describe reused computation and are part of the evidence.

The frozen study uses original local Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct in BF16, fixed B8/L224/cap64, full recomputation with cache disabled, and actual final RMSNorm/unembedding in FP32. Source is `695b126a9d5fe3045c9b5872fac7441d8fcdeec2`. The 24 previously exposed historical confirmation stories supply four P/N×truth inputs each (96 fresh baselines), eight directed pairs per story (192), and 20 arms per pair: **3,936 planned and accepted EOS completions**. Calibration's 180 rows remain excluded. There was no behavioral-correctness or deception-specificity gate.

U5={(12,6),(13,5),(13,20),(13,21),(14,24)}; H26={(17,26)}; U6=U5∪H26. These and H24 are contained in S32=D∩H∩F; S32 is a larger overlapping benchmark, not an additive residual decomposition. H25/H27 are **not** in S32. H26 is a same-layer output contribution and cannot cause layer 17 Q/K/V states. Q17H24 is one head; K/V17group6 supplies query heads 24–27. K17group0 supplies heads 0–3 outside D∪H∪F, without any presumed inertness. All MLPs and unselected components evolve naturally; this is not an isolated D-only circuit.

Donor head outputs replace only the saved common mapped original-prefix coordinates. U5-induced receivers are captured in that freely evolving recipient pass. Clean restoration replaces an interface with its natural recipient baseline; induced rescue transfers its U5-induced state into a baseline recipient with no U5 source replacement. Receiver banks cover every original recipient prefix position and remain in recipient coordinates, Q/K are pre-RoPE, output slices are pre-o_proj, and generated/padded/dummy positions are never replaced.
