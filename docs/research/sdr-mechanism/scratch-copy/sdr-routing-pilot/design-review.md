## Scientific interpretation and constraints

There are three separate findings: a causal effect of a particular activation interchange, a donor-answer/content-transfer diagnostic, and pressure-dependent mediation. Only the first is automatic from a successful patch. The second requires crossed observed answer support. The third additionally requires relevant matched pressure contrasts and convergent path/control evidence; changing donor truth to hold its emitted answer fixed leaves a truth-content alternative. Even natural same-answer matched pairs concern a selected overlap stratum and do not identify an average pressure effect over all contingent stories.

Truth accessibility must remain an observed behavioral check on the same record under H/N, with nonaccessible rows retained and reported. Correct forced-choice retrieval does not establish latent belief. Semantic alignment must preserve item identity and mention role, never merely token position; removing pressure means the changed clause has no mapped source. All-token and common-segment patches estimate different interventions and must stay separate. Preserve the historical missing-space suffix rather than silently regenerating the scaffold.

Discovery/confirmation separation is prospective only: candidate heads and source survivor filtering already use these datasets. Split by base story before model screening, prohibit related truth/pressure/order variants crossing splits, freeze donor choice independent of patch results, report support and effects separately by split, and keep cluster-bootstrap interpretation descriptive at this sample size. No pressure search/top-up after seeing outcomes.

Two reserved H200 hours is a hard cap including failed allocations. First allocation <=1h, no second before written gate, second <=1h. Run calibration and baseline support first; save an interpretable insufficient-support result if mapping, numerical equality, emission identity, or support fails. Stage 2 must shrink optional position cells before risking incomplete core controls. No paid calls, model downloads, training, fresh EVAL5, v2 circuit evaluations, or intent claims.
