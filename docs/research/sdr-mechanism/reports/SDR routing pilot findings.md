# SDR pilot identifies answer-processing pathways

The pilot narrows how these heads affect SDR answers: **candidate-head perturbations reach a layer-17 key-input route and a smaller head-specific query route**, with effects recurring on frozen confirmation stories. The candidate family also changes answers when its donor emits the same false item, ruling out simple emitted-item copying as a universal explanation. But these findings do not identify a pure pressure signal or a complete deception mechanism. Shared-head replacement can improve pressured answers and severely impair honest answers; selective suppression remains unestablished.

Both approved stages are complete. No further experiments were run or are proposed here. The [original mechanism review](</projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/project_context/reports/SDR circuit mechanism review.md>) provides context. Detailed estimates and every fixed path, including nulls and reversals, are preserved in the [Stage 1 analysis](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage1-analysis.md) and [Stage 2 analysis](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage2-analysis.md).

## Candidate effects extend beyond donor-answer copying

The frozen pilot used **48 stories**, split 24 discovery/24 confirmation, with 480 baseline variants. Stage 1 completed 6,528 partial-position patches; 384 proposals lacked fixed cross-story partners and were explicitly excluded. All 1,149 self/empty numerical receipts passed bitwise equality and mapping checks. P denotes pressure, H an added honest instruction, and N pressure removal. Every MLP remained intact; ordinary family patches let the recipient remainder evolve freely. ([Stage 1](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage1-analysis.md))

In changed-truth H/N→P comparisons where donors emit the recipient's **same false item**, candidate6 (the six tested sender heads) improves the recipient truth-minus-other-item margin by **+1.71 [1.35, 2.07]**, across 76 rows/48 stories. Selector2 (the two nominated receiver heads) has a corresponding effect of **+.042 [−.059, .141]**. Candidate6 therefore does more than impose that donor item, whereas selector2 shows little effect in this particular donor stratum. Changing designation/truth also changes content, preventing a pure-pressure interpretation. These family names are candidate labels, not established semantic roles. ([Emission-stratified evidence](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage1-analysis.md))

## Focused paths recur through key and query inputs

Stage 2 completed all **51,136 planned rows across 39 fixed paths and family interactions**, with no unmeasured rows; 1,088 parent-baseline checksum checks and numerical controls passed. Calibration rows were excluded from inference. Analysis followed the [pre-result interpretation rubric](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage2-analysis-plan.md).

In the same-false-donor diagnostic, confirmation includes **41 rows/24 stories**. Common-position sender injections yield truth-margin changes of **+.201 [.157, .243] for L13H20→L17Kgroup6**, **+.181 [.141, .219] for L13H21→L17Kgroup6**, and **+.147 [.110, .186] for L12H6→L17Kgroup6**. All have positive discovery effects and persist separately for H and N donors. Kgroup6 supplies keys to query heads **24–27**, so these are not uniquely L17H24 effects. Head-specific evidence is **L13H5→L17H24Q: +.096 [.073, .121]** on confirmation. ([Complete path evidence](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage2-analysis.md))

The three leading key effects nearly vanish with readout-only sender injection; the L13H5 query effect remains. A smaller value route also exists, so V is not absent. These results locate interfaces carrying the tested perturbations, compatible with an attention-selection contribution. Key groups and individual query heads differ in intervention size. Direct paths freeze other components during sender capture; ordinary family effects do not. Their magnitudes cannot be summed or divided into mediated fractions. ([Scope and estimands](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage2-analysis.md))

## Joint replacement interferes, and honest collateral remains

In the diagnostic confirmation stratum, candidate6 alone gives **+1.762 [1.364, 2.179]**, selector2 alone **+.061 [−.090, .218]**, and their union **+1.082 [.745, 1.454]**. The interaction is **−.740 [−.909, −.573]**, across 41 rows/24 stories. Candidate/outsider replacement also interferes. Replacing a downstream family's output can overwrite a state that would otherwise respond to candidate injection. Thus these results establish conditional interference under replacement, not natural competition, serial mediation, backups, or redundancy. ([Interaction audit](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage2-analysis.md))

Shared32, the 32-head D∩H∩F shared mask, illustrates the collateral tradeoff. Same-truth H→P replacement restores truth in **34/48** false recipients, but P→H loses **36/37** accessible truths. Half-dose shared32 reduces honest/no-pressure truth losses to **12/37 and 7/39**, versus full-dose **36/37 and 35/39**, while also reducing corrective margins. One partial dose does not establish an efficacy–utility frontier. Coherent refusal remains desired suppression success, but this first-token forced-choice pilot did not evaluate refusal or general utility. ([Directional and dose results](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage2-analysis.md))

## What remains unresolved

Confirmation stays within previously selected native SDR data used to discover masks. Intervals are exploratory pointwise 95% story-bootstrap intervals, unadjusted across paths/strata. Content-confounded donors prevent pure-pressure identification; untested MLP, multi-hop, and position routes prevent completeness claims. There is no external/model replication, strategic-intent evidence, or demonstrated selective suppression. ([Scientific limits](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/sdr-routing-pilot/stage2-analysis.md))
