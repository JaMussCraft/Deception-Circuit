# Native SDR controls fail the prospective validation

**The matched-control study failed its frozen validation criteria, despite a consequence-related effect in the prespecified conflicting cases.** Neutral reporting and factual retrieval were below their capability thresholds, and the effect depended strongly on wording and truth/order. This assay cannot support the intended circuit interpretation. No new causal grid follows from these results.

The outcome-independent panel crossed 32 fixtures, two clause wordings, truth, item order, favored identity, three consequence conditions and reporting versus factual retrieval: **3,072 responses**, all retained. The contingent condition threatened a weights wipe depending on the submitted item; the adverse control applied the same event unconditionally, and the neutral control ruled it out. The endpoint was choosing the non-designated result to show, which is not automatically a false factual assertion.

**Capability failed before any pathway interpretation.** Neutral reports selected the designated result in **337/512 (65.8%)**, below the required 90%. Retrieval was correct in **1,392/1,536 (90.6%)**, below 95%. Another **24 of 80** prespecified capability blocks failed. Overall, **30 of 105** fixed checks failed; these are not 105 independent statistical tests. Favorable pooled contrasts cannot override these failures.

In conflicting reporting cases, non-designated choices were **142/256 contingent**, **118/256 unconditional adverse**, and **94/256 neutral**. Contingent-minus-adverse was **+9.4 percentage points**, below the required 15; contingent-minus-neutral was **+18.8 points**. Their simultaneous 95% intervals were **[+3.9,+14.8]** and **[+13.3,+24.2] points**, conditional on the designed fixtures. Across all reporting cases, contingent and unconditional adverse conditions each produced **207/512 non-designated choices**; the quoted contrasts refer to prespecified conflicting cases. Positive effects occur within those cases, but capability and robustness criteria fail. Wording B gave **−3.1 and +7.0 points**, both below its 10-point requirements; one truth/order block had zero contingent-minus-adverse effect. Retrieval deterioration guards passed, but absolute retrieval capability still failed.

Both independent blinded annotators covered all **332 exact unique views**; a third blinded pass adjudicated six disagreements, restoring all original row weights. All responses ended at EOS, with no caps/errors/missing rows; numerical controls passed. A complete-text audit of all **589 non-designated reporting responses** found simple one-candidate commitments without additional factual disclosure. That observation does not establish omission, intent, audience deception or a false assertion.

The earlier [pilot's conditional QK interfaces](SDR%20routing%20pilot%20findings.md) remain useful hypotheses, and the [completion diagnostic](SDR%20routing%20validation%20findings.md) remains a separate format finding. For this setup, the priority is a reliable behavioral contrast with capable controls before richer circuit tools or head-role claims. Outcome-independent selection did not create an unexposed or OOD panel: the base universe was historically screened.
