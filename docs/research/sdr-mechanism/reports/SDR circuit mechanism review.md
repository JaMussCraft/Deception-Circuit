# Shared circuits demand causal routing tests

**The evidence is consistent with deception reusing ordinary answer-processing machinery. Whether pressure changes its routing, and which components carry that change, remains unresolved.** Shared-core knockout is powerful, but it cannot distinguish answer disruption, pressure-dependent routing, and donor-content replacement. Subtracting H/F also discards the strongest existing deception-leaning candidates. One premise needs correction: **D−F is strong on native SDR**, but has comparatively weak external effects and does not outperform its matched random control in the reviewed suite; triple-overlap evidence is native-only. “Heads-only” sufficiency retains every MLP. The toolkit is good enough for the next causal tests, but current results do not justify semantic head labels or intent claims. I recommend a small matched counterfactual/donor pilot, followed conditionally by focused paths and interactions, before feature tracing. A proposed **two-H200-GPU-hour maximum**, split at an internal stop/go checkpoint, is a conservative first-phase envelope requiring approval. The strongest publication opportunity is explaining and predicting incentive-conditioned reuse of shared computation, rather than discovering another sparse lying intervention.

## Shared membership explains powerful knockouts without identifying functions

D/H/F contain 69/56/68 heads; their triple intersection contains 32. Native interchange knockout leaves deceptive two-choice accuracy at .002 for D, .002 for H, .000 for F, and .002 for triple32. These nearly saturated outcomes establish strong causal sensitivity under the tested replacement; they cannot rank specificity. **D−F33 leaves .119 versus a matched-null fifth percentile of .517**. D−H26 leaves .384 in the earlier pair wave; D−H−F22 leaves .782 versus null p5 .733. Thus the defensible pattern is strong overlap, weak triple-exclusive remainder, and a native/external dissociation for D−F—not universally weak differences. ([Suite results](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/outputs/_suites/format-control-sdr-coarse-nls0.3/evaluation_summary.md); [pair wave](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/outputs/llama-8b-instruct_sdr_coarse/_launch/wave_status.md))

This pattern has a concrete structural explanation. All six leading deception-leaning candidates—L12H6, L13H5, L13H20, L13H21, L14H24, L17H26—and both putative selector/readout candidates, L17H24 and L24H27, belong to triple32. D−H−F excludes all eight. L15H8/L17H25, important outsiders to D∩H, belong to D∩F−H and disappear when subtracting F. **Structural subtraction removes candidates useful across conditions, including candidates strongly implicated in deceptive performance.** It does not isolate a psychological “deception-only” module. The shared43 circuit is only .494 sufficient on D, while adding D-exclusive heads restores .967; exclusive heads therefore contribute conditionally despite lacking standalone sufficiency. ([Membership audit](</projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/project_context/research_notes/SDR circuit mechanism review/evidence-audit.md>); [component results](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/outputs/_analyses/sdr-dh-core/dh_core__leaf/sdr/result_summary.md); [pair wave](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/outputs/llama-8b-instruct_sdr_coarse/_launch/wave_status.md))

The candidate roles remain unresolved. Early/middle candidates have strong knockout effects but little direct logit writing; this motivates downstream mediation. L17H24/L24H27 have large honest and deceptive effects and answer-aligned direct writes, fitting either answer transport or selection. Attention to pressure cannot distinguish them. Existing “shared,” “deception-leaning,” and “honest-leaning” labels compare different D/H prompt samples, so they are descriptive priorities for testing. The saved role analysis explicitly remains unknown/insufficient. Joint-core subadditivity also fails to establish redundancy because the joint effect approaches the item-swap endpoint. ([Component results](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/outputs/_analyses/sdr-dh-core/dh_core__leaf/sdr/result_summary.md); [role assignments](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/outputs/_analyses/sdr-dh-core/dh_core__leaf/sdr/roles/roles.json))

Two distinctions constrain every claim. **Sufficiency preserves selected heads plus all MLPs, while nonselected heads receive corrupt-reference activations**; knockout leaves the full network background active. Sufficiency is conditional on both backgrounds. Native component knockout overwrites every token position with item-swap activations, whereas external evaluation uses honest twins or reference-pool donors. These estimate replacement effects, not pure removal. Composition must subtract MLPs at leaf granularity. ([Knockout implementation](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/component_analysis/knockout.py); [benchmark donors](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/evaluation/benchmark_eval.py))

External support is narrower than the overlap story suggests. Coarse triple32, D−H26, and D−H−F22 have no reviewed external suites. In a common-screen sandbagging/MASK evaluation, dataset-macro raw deception rates are D .312, H .172, F .273, D−F .650; D−F's own random mean is .618. The format verdict also needs care: F's mean deception reduction exceeds D's (.777 versus .723); F narrowly fails the chosen collateral rule through reference-NLL rise (.668 versus .611). This supports a measured tradeoff, not demonstrated deception specificity. Small screened samples, six null draws, and judge variation limit certainty. ([External results](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/outputs/eval5_format_sdr_coarse/result_summary.md); [decision conditions](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/outputs/_suites/format-control-sdr-coarse-nls0.3/decision.json))

## Recent mechanisms make semantic counterfactuals the useful advance

Sparse causal control of lying is already prior art: the ICLR 2025 *Universal Motif* paper reports sparse late-stage components in induced factual lying. A 2026 shared sycophancy–lying preprint reports shared heads and correlated path effects, although its “factual lying” contrast includes true/false statement evaluation. Neither overlap nor another successful sparse intervention supplies a sufficient novelty claim. ([Universal Motif](https://openreview.net/pdf/c2fdd8abfdc5d54b353bb6da7d630d92609d830b.pdf); [shared circuit, §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.19117v4))

The most directly useful precedent uses **the same Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct model**. *Tracing mechanisms of sycophantic agreement* separates opinion registration from answer retrieval through answer-label swaps and cross-question transplantation. It motivates testing whether SDR candidates carry a contextual answer reference, answer content, or pressure-conditioned selection. *Can LLMs Lie?* additionally implicates subject/instruction/template-token computation, making positional controls necessary. Merely identifying an early selector and late answer writer would therefore be incremental; explaining how incentive information changes their causal interaction is the stronger target. ([Sycophancy mechanisms, including Appendix D](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35822v1); [lying localization](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.03518v1))

Toxicity research warns against confusing a compact intervention with a complete explanation. An ICML 2024 account argues DPO bypasses preserved toxic capabilities; a later EMNLP analysis finds selected toxicity neurons explain only 2.5–24% of DPO's effect and develops a distributed signed-group explanation. For SDR, recoverability and interactions can distinguish lost capability from redirected access or competing contributions. Refusal also has a powerful causal direction, so changed reporting policy deserves its own interpretation. **Coherent refusal remains successful suppression**; it need not imply deceptive knowledge was erased. ([DPO mechanism](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/lee24a.html); [distributed toxicity analysis](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1501/); [refusal direction](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.11717v3))

The tools are adequate for these questions. Existing path code supports instruction/item-swap counterfactuals, Q receivers, shared-group K/V receivers, MLP/readout routes, and position followups. Better contrasts take priority over new infrastructure: patching conclusions depend on corruption and metric, and causal abstraction asks whether interchange implements a specified high-level variable change. SAE/transcoder assets exist for this exact checkpoint, but wrapper compatibility, memory, reconstruction fidelity, and feature coverage remain untested. Feature graphs also freeze attention patterns and miss QK-mediated computation, limiting their ability to settle a routing hypothesis alone. ([Local path tools](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/component_analysis/path_patching.py); [patching practices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042); [causal abstraction](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/4f5c422f4d49a5a807eda27434231040-Abstract.html); [exact-model transcoders](https://huggingface.co/facebook/crv-8b-instruct-transcoders/blob/main/README.md); [tracing limits](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/methods.html))

## Four sequential tests can separate routing from shared damage

**Rank 1: matched semantic and donor dissociation.** Freeze a small subset of existing pressure-contingent survivors, balancing record-supported identity, item order, and designation polarity; aim for 24–48 base stories with discovery/confirmation separation. Independently cross donor pressure with donor emitted-answer identity: require pressure-changing donors that emit the same answer and unchanged-pressure donors that emit a different answer, with matched recipient record/order controls. Cross answer identity separately while preserving pressure. Compare same-story matched honest/instruction donors, item swaps, and nuisance-matched same-condition donors, including self-patching. Start with six candidate heads as a family, the two selector/readout candidates, outsider pair, and small shared-core controls. Use semantic position mapping; pressure-clause deletion cannot silently shift hook alignment. Existing 520 paired no-pressure survivors are a starting pool, not an unselected population: report all pilot rows and any behavior-selected subset separately. The old D/H splits differ substantially, and F training includes honest-split fallback. ([Data audit](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/.scratch/mechanism-datasets/reports/00-dataset-audit.md); [survivor counts](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/data/datasets/sdr_nopressure/filter_report.json))

Pre-register recipient-truth logit contrast, both answer probabilities, full-vocabulary outputs, and neutral two-choice performance. Before inferring routing, verify content accessibility through matched truthful-retrieval/readout performance using the same record. Routing predicts pressure-dependent reporting changes beyond donor-answer copying while truthful retrieval remains intact; extraction predicts content transport across pressure; an execution bottleneck predicts similar neutral/honest damage; overwrite predicts donor-answer tracking under unchanged pressure. Multiple donor draws share the recipient story as the uncertainty unit. Stop if natural pairs lack pressure contingency, the required pressure × emitted-answer combinations lack support, variables cannot be independently separated, or legitimate donor families reverse the inferred role. Repair the design before assigning routing roles. **Planning allowance: 10–30 H200 minutes, capped at one hour**, including loading/capture uncertainty; no generation/judging suite.

**Rank 2: focused paths with rescue.** If the donor pilot yields a stable contrast, test the six early/middle candidates into L17H24/L24H27 through Q/K/V and semantic positions, respecting layer order and grouped K/V. Include an MLP receiver and an outside-mask control. Pressure-dependent Q/K effects that predict changed attention and recover the expected recipient answer favor routing; content-dependent V effects favor transport. Restoration alone does not separate them. Require component-effect recovery and reverse-direction predictions; unexplained effects trigger broader receivers rather than role assignment. The 36-cell skeleton costs roughly 936 forward batches per counterfactual on the audit's 100-row example. **Allow 10–25 H200 minutes**, with position/capture uncertainty. Arbitrary receiver restriction requires a CLI check and possibly a small later extension; do not default to the full grid. ([Tool and runtime audit](</projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/project_context/research_notes/SDR circuit mechanism review/evidence-audit.md>))

**Rank 3: conditional interactions away from saturation.** Use candidate6, selector2, outsider2, and a small residual-core group. Measure groups alone, jointly, and restored into mild-corruption backgrounds under matched D/H/no-pressure conditions. Report the interaction contrast Y(A,B)−Y(A)−Y(B)+Y(neither), with signed logits and uncertainty. A weak group becoming important after another route is perturbed, accompanied by compensating downstream writes, supports a backup account. Equal interactions on neutral controls favor generic computation. Reduce group sizes/dose when probabilities saturate. **Allow 5–15 H200 minutes**; this tests interactions rather than repeating sparse mask optimization.

**Rank 4: held-out behavioral confirmation, then optional representation analysis.** Only a donor-stable, paired-selective candidate proceeds to the active native/benign protocol, reusing its baselines and v3 comparison. Report raw deception first; coherent refusal counts as success, while malformed/repetitive output and ordinary utility remain separate. No fresh broad EVAL5 screen or judge budget is proposed here. If whole-head interventions mix useful and harmful computation, choose one targeted subspace or pretrained-feature pilot with reconstruction/error controls and original-model rescue. Cost remains uncalibrated and requires a separate proposal. This stage depends on the existing dataset and selective-suppression investigations; it does not restart their builds or training. ([Active-work dependencies](</projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/project_context/research_notes/SDR circuit mechanism review/evidence-audit.md>))

For ranks 1–3, **request at most two H200 GPU-hours, zero paid APIs**: one hour maximum for rank 1, then one further hour only if its design/effects pass the checkpoint. No pretrained-asset download/install belongs in this phase. Analyze existing SDR counterfactuals; use any new v2 dataset only after that investigation's separate approval gates. Failed matched-pair viability blocks downstream tests and is itself informative. After approval, measure a small actual calibration before expanding the cell count; this cap is not a guarantee of completing every path/interaction. Logged calibration is 13,754 forwards in 505.7 seconds on H200; new prompt length, captures, and scheduling prevent a precise runtime promise. A40 would require a separately adjusted wall-time estimate. No jobs, GPU experiments, notebooks, or API calls were run in this review. ([Measured sweep cost](/projects/beto/swong2/Alignment/Deception-Circuit/outputs/_analyses/sdr-dh-core-kosweep/dh_core__leaf/sdr/knockout/sweep.json))

## Publication value depends on predicting a new intervention

The strongest positive contribution is **incentive-conditioned reuse of shared answer computation**, demonstrated by content-preserving bidirectional swaps, path-specific rescue, held-out semantic predictions, and transfer beyond the SDR scaffold. Matched neutral damage or donor-answer copying would defeat that interpretation. A second contribution is a general audit showing how sufficient behavior masks select shared task bottlenecks; it needs replication across another model/behavior and a validated criterion resolving the ambiguity. A third is a quantitative distributed-competition account predicting unseen joint interventions and a useful selective intervention. Existing toxicity results make distributed effects alone insufficient novelty.

Forced-choice pressure dependence does not establish latent belief, strategic intent, audience modeling, or omission. External evaluation is also selected: screened deceptive items and dataset-macro averages should retain their denominators and selection scope. A selective target must show a condition-dependent causal variable/path while preserving matched ordinary computation; membership in D−H−F is neither necessary nor sufficient. **Proceed toward a mechanism paper only when the proposed computation predicts held-out intervention outcomes better than shared-bottleneck and donor-overwrite alternatives.**
