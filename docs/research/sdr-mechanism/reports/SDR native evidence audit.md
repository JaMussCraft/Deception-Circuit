This outcome-conditioned diagnostic separates change from pre-existing agreement. Among 232 same-emission pairs, candidate6 changes the answer in **44**, refuting universal emitted-answer copying. Every counterexample changes facts; fixed-fact cases change **0/40**. Missing full condition×answer crossing limits full-factorial pressure identification without erasing valid clause-edit comparisons.
