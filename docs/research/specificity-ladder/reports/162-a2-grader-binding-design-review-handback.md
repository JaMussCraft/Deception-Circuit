## How verified

Read the raw design and exact baseline A2 live-validator, manifest/read-stage, original grader observer and current registry guard code. No author outcome narrative was used. The original held-out guards protect instance IDs and referenced ID-file integrity, but do not enforce foreign catalogue path declarations. The old whole-catalogue hash incidentally stopped every such update; an unconditional foreign-row exemption would have lost that stop. [Boundary and source hashes](162-a2-grader-binding-design-review-evidence/verdict.json).

R3 preserves exact own entry at each lexical canonical route, all nonempty foreign protective declarations as a duplicate-preserving multiset, and complete authoritative top-level content. Only the explicit valid top `updated` timestamp and known nonprotective foreign metadata may vary. Broad historical declarations stay bound without interpreting globs or adding an overlap engine. Same-buffer hash/parse, strict typed canonical content and original live grader checks remain mandatory. [Binding semantics](162-a2-grader-binding-design-review-evidence/reviewed-design.json).
