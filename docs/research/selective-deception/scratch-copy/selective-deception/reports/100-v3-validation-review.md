# V3 validation scientific and selection review

PASS for prospective finalist freeze; no scientific selection blocker. This is a review of the protocol and recorded validation histories, not an independent GPU replay or source-integrity certification. The proposal remains nonauthoritative: root must write the final receipt before heldout assessment.

I independently recomputed eligibility and ranking from all 96 validation checkpoints (four runs, each 24 checkpoints through update 1536). There are 68 nonempty positive-suppression checkpoints, 48 meeting WikiText limits, and zero meeting joint preservation. No constrained checkpoint reaches either 95% or 90% target rung. The frozen fallback therefore selects union-W update 1344, followed by the strongest distinct unconstrained mask, all-W update 1536. This matches selection-proposal.json, including its tier labels and two-finalist maximum. The decision uses validation only; historical W9 is a comparison reference, not a selection input.

Baseline H is 86/119; the finalists yield 80/119 and 85/119. Almost unchanged net H accuracy for all-W conceals five losses among initially correct examples. WikiText limits are PPL increase at most 5% (mean NLL increase at most log(1.05)) and KL at most 0.05. The all-head mask includes 24 heads outside the 103-head union, so expansion discovers an alternative intervention, but its stronger suppression costs more heads and exceeds the frozen collateral limits.

The high-level result is a modest improvement in the validation suppression frontier, without a jointly preserving solution. Historical W9's roughly 55% surrogate reduction versus the new union mask's 59% is descriptive development-set progress, not fresh evidence of better deception suppression. All-W reaches 65%, still well short of either requested target rung. These percentages concern mean -log(1-p(deceptive-label-set)); they are neither deceptive-response rates nor equivalent percentage changes in probability. SDR argmax changes are likewise task-label results, not annotations of intent or natural-language deception.

Interpretation remains bounded by one seed, reused development validation, rho=0 (no explicit sparsity penalty), honest-donor content replacement, and unchanged open MLP/background circuitry. The results establish neither a minimal necessary set nor a localized strategic-deception mechanism. Failure to find joint preservation in this fixed search does not prove that no such circuit exists. The 28-head endpoint being best does not by itself justify another training grid.

The next useful inference is the already planned same-protocol heldout comparison of intact, old sufficientD69, old necessaryW9, and the two frozen masks. Apply identical removal polarity and donor replay to all masks: knocking out the old sufficient set tests its removal effect, not its original sufficiency claim. Report fresh SDR surrogate and paired H transitions separately from native false endorsements, coherent refusals, and truthful responses, alongside WikiText loss/KL. A coherent D refusal is successful behavioral suppression; an H refusal is a utility loss. Keep malformed/empty outputs separate. This comparison can show whether the validation gain transfers beyond answer-label routing and whether it improves on the old circuits at acceptable collateral cost; it cannot establish autonomous strategic deception from an instructed-lying slice alone. No new experimental grid is recommended before that comparison.
