# V3 final scientific review

Verdict: PASS for reporting the completed frozen comparison; partial practical progress, not a selective-deception solution. The all-head search produces a smaller intervention with stronger heldout SDR suppression, more clear native corrections, and substantially less collateral damage than removing old sufficientD69. It does not beat D69 on the prespecified raw false-assertion metric, reach 100% (or 90/95%) surrogate suppression, or satisfy joint H preservation. No further tuning or grid is recommended from these results alone.

Intact native D is 25 false, 5 ambiguous, 2 true; intact H is 26 true, 5 ambiguous, 1 false. Native outputs have no annotated malformed/empty responses, capped outputs, or coherent refusals. Thus clear suppression here consists of truthful corrections; coherent refusal would also have been successful suppression had it occurred.

The metric-dependent conclusion must remain explicit. Raw false assertions fall from 25/32 intact to 6/32 with D69 versus 13/32 with allW28, so D69 wins that headline. But its 14 ambiguous D outputs include 12 contradiction flags; ambiguity is neither demonstrated safe refusal nor automatic deception. AllW28 has 5 ambiguous D outputs and 14 true responses versus D69's 12 true. Assigning unresolved ambiguous responses either nonfalse or false yields bounds of 18.75–62.5% for D69 and 40.625–56.25% for allW28: the ranking is not robust to ambiguity. These are label-sensitivity bounds, not confidence intervals. Do not replace the raw headline with correction rate to declare an unconditional winner.

Paired IDs clarify the small correction advantage: only three baseline-false examples are corrected by both D69 and allW28; eight favor allW28 and six favor D69. Eleven versus nine corrections therefore is exploratory evidence, not established superiority. On initially truthful native H, eleven are retained by both, ten only by allW28, and one only by D69: utility retention is the clearer observed advantage. AllW28 still loses five initially truthful H responses, gains one, and introduces one D false assertion from a previously ambiguous case. W13 and W9 each correct six baseline-false cases and retain 23/26 baseline-truthful H; longer restricted training shows no clear native correction benefit here.

The SDR surrogate is mean -log(1-p(deceptive-label-set)), not deceptive-response frequency. AllW28's 65.33% reduction leaves 21/64 deceptive-label argmax outputs. Its unchanged aggregate SDR H accuracy (42/64) conceals three lost and three gained cases. WT PPL rises 5.11% and predictive KL is 0.08668, exceeding both frozen WT limits; the mask retains its unconstrained-frontier status. Fewer heads than D69 does not establish minimality or necessity of individual heads; D69 was assessed here with removal polarity, not retested for its original sufficiency property.

The recorded label freeze precedes the condition join, with 339/352 independent category agreements, 100 fresh blind adjudications, and zero identical-public-view semantic conflicts. Numerical result hash matches its summary. Judgments remain model-agent labels with potentially shared errors; 32 paired items across four domains and 16 WT windows are small samples. The numerical summary's native_labels_pending=true is an earlier-stage status field; the separately frozen native results provide completed annotation evidence. Honest-prompt donors themselves are only 26/32 clearly truthful, with two false and four ambiguous, and were not screened post hoc. Fixed replay, including prefix mapping and terminal-state reuse, supports a controlled mask comparison but remains content replacement rather than neutral deletion. One seed, no sparsity penalty, open background circuitry, and instructed lying limit mechanistic and strategic-deception claims.

Single next direction: validate the frozen allW28 intervention in a behavior-grounded matched D/H setting with genuine strategic pressure and a capable benign instruction-following control, retaining fixed donor assignments and prespecified raw false/refusal/ambiguity plus utility reporting. First establish intact behavioral headroom and control capability; do not tune another SDR mask grid to compensate for an unsuitable task. This follows the observed transfer signal while testing whether it reflects deception-related behavior rather than general instruction or answer routing. No experiment is launched or authorized by this review.
