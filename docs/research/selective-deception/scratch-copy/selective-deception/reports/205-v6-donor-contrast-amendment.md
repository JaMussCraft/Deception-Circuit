## Estimand and claim boundary

Retain the paired primary readout **recipient-truth margin(ordinal agree)−recipient-truth margin(ordinal disagree)** on all16cases, with all D/H/F/I requested-margin/accuracy changes and original-donor anchors. A positive pattern across variants is consistent with donor-associated answer-position routing even when words differ; it is not evidence that donor facts or lexical answers were copied. Preserve existing full-vocabulary argmax IDs and compare them descriptively with the explicit donor/recipient token identities so donor-word intrusion, recipient-option choice and other outputs are not conflated. Do not create a new success label from this comparison.

Crucially, the two arms use different donors whose words, facts, lengths and activations can differ. Matching ordinal slots does not experimentally isolate ordinal position from all these differences. A nonzero effect establishes sensitivity to these frozen donor assignments and supports a position-routing hypothesis for later tests; it does not prove a pure positional code, deception specificity, factual content transfer or a mediated fraction of the full mask effect. A null is likewise confined to this donor pool/readout intervention. Lexical-match stratification is descriptive only, with no claim of powered subgroup inference.

This is a prospective, explicit narrowing of the causal question before outcomes, not a recovery of the unavailable exact-vocabulary experiment. It keeps the complete matched recipient panel and provides useful mechanism evidence without a new pool, mask search, API call or giant control grid.
