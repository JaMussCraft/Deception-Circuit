New23 versus new30 tests the joint conditional effect of removing those seven heads while the other 23 are already removed. This is neither a seven-head standalone circuit nor seven individual causal attributions. Do not automatically add seven-only, individual addbacks, new masks or donor sweeps to EVAL 5. ## Separate V2 transfer hypothesis

Question: does a fixed removal learned from the older SDR task reduce pressure-conditioned deceptive answer preference on independently constructed FAR/SDR/SER V2 rows while preserving benign correctness? This is an out-of-training-distribution transfer test of the fixed support, not proof of a pure or motive-driven deception mechanism.

Use fixed new30, new23 and intact first on paired D, neutral-clause honest, and clause-deleted nopressure variants for all three tasks. D69 can be added as the historical comparator if the explicit question is improved selectivity versus D69; the six V2-trained masks are not needed for this initial test. V2 `_honest` means a token-count-matched neutral-clause twin; the explicit honest-instruction variant is a different control and must not be substituted silently. Preserve row correspondence and report inversion/identity and clause-family strata. Ensure the selected split and source instances have not been used for mask adaptation; disclose builder filtering on the intact model. V2 benchmark rows are construction-filtered survivors, not an estimate of prevalence in unrestricted prompts.

Measure D deceptive-label probability/margin and answer-choice accuracy; paired H/nopressure correct-label probability, accuracy and preservation of baseline-correct responses; and the pressure-versus-control change in these effects. Report raw task-specific effects before any combined contrast. A pressure-specific margin/accuracy change with benign preservation supports selective transfer within this scaffold. Similar damage in D and benign controls supports generic task disruption. Neither pattern by itself establishes semantic lying in free generation. A local forced-choice evaluation can use construction labels with zero paid judge calls; semantic free-generation evaluation is a different deliverable and needs separately costed grading.

V2 improves contingency and control matching, but all three tasks remain rule-instructed. FAR uses the stronger act-naming subset while SDR/SER use a mixed pool. Purity is construction-defined; the two-rater one-word judge failed SDR/SER separation (kappa 0.00/0.08). Do not call these clean strategic-deception datasets or interpret cross-task effect differences as pure mechanism differences. Training-gate passes and the six new trained masks do not remove these limitations. Keep this transfer test distinct from the already authorized mechanism-datasets Stage 1 evaluations and its separately gated Stage 2.
