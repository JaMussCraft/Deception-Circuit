## Review focus

- Old timing config/release/CPU proof cannot authorize full_score: Task1/Task3 reject them even if hashes and source are genuine.
- Stale or mutating bank/aliases/catalogues during long prechecks stop entry: Task1/Task2/Task3 pins and entry-bound negative tests.

- Unsafe resource defaults or active A3 reader during mapping transition cannot be masked by passing score science: Task1/Task3 require explicit reviewed resource and cutover proofs.
- Different GPU UUID must not reject authentic bank reuse or conceal a numerical/name mismatch: Task1/Task2 verify exact contract/name/environment and record both UUIDs independently.
